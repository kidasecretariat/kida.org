About KIDA

Building Korea’s Debate Community

KIDA supports English-language parliamentary debate through tournaments, education, leadership, and intervarsity collaboration.

Who We Are

Established In 2006

KIDA is a representative body of Korean university students engaged in English-language parliamentary debate.

The organization consists of Council members from each university’s debate society/institution alongside the Secretariat. KIDA provides training sessions, monthly Council meetings, semester tournaments, and supervision of projects conducted by societies. The KIDA Council also discusses and advances important issues and agendas for the debating community of Korea.
